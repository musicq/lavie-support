Automatic logging

Pay with Apple Pay or get a bank alert, and the purchase lands in Lavie by itself, filed under the name's category.

It takes one shortcut, added once, and one automation per source in the Shortcuts app. Lavie reads each alert on your iPhone and sends you a note you can tap to fix or swipe to undo. Codes, declined cards and balance alerts are ignored.

1. Add the Lavie shortcut

Open this link on your iPhone with Lavie installed, then tap Add Shortcut. It appears in Shortcuts as "Log in Lavie". That's the only shortcut you need; every source below runs it.

Add the Lavie shortcut

If Safari shows a download instead, open the file from the Downloads icon and it opens in Shortcuts.

2. Choose what runs it

Each source is an automation in the Shortcuts app: open Shortcuts, tap Automation at the bottom, then +. Pick the trigger, then the shortcut you just added. Choose Run Immediately so it never asks.

Bank or wallet app notifications (iOS 27 and later)

Card purchases, PayNow, Alipay, WeChat Pay: whatever your apps already notify you about.

  1. Shortcuts › Automation › + › Notification.
  2. Choose the bank or wallet app. Leave the text filter empty. Choose Run Immediately, then Next.
  3. Under My Shortcuts, pick Log in Lavie. Done.

Repeat for each app you want to read. A notification that isn't about a purchase logs nothing.

Apple Pay (iOS 17 and later)

The amount and the shop, the moment you tap your iPhone or Apple Watch. Only payments made by tapping; online purchases arrive through your bank's notifications instead.

  1. Shortcuts › Automation › + › Transaction (called Wallet on newer iOS).
  2. Pick the cards you pay with. Choose Run Immediately, then Next.
  3. Under My Shortcuts, pick Log in Lavie. Done.

Bank text messages

The SMS alerts many banks send for every card charge.

  1. Shortcuts › Automation › + › Message.
  2. Under Message Contains, type a phrase your bank's alerts always use, such as "transaction of", "消费" or "ご利用". Bank numbers usually can't be chosen as a sender, so the phrase is what picks the right messages. Choose Run Immediately, then Next.
  3. Under My Shortcuts, pick Log in Lavie. Done.

Card alerts by email

Cards that email you after each charge, as many do in Japan. Works with the Mail app.

  1. Shortcuts › Automation › + › Email.
  2. Pick the sender your card alerts come from. Choose Run Immediately, then Next.
  3. Under My Shortcuts, pick Log in Lavie. Done.

3. Check it reads your alerts

In Lavie, open Settings › Automatic logging › Try it, paste a real alert from your bank, and tap Read it. If Lavie reads it wrong, send us the alert's wording (with the amounts changed) and we'll teach it. You can also turn on Ask DeepSeek to read it for alerts Lavie can't read on its own: only that alert's text is sent, ten times a cycle on the free plan.

One tap, no automation

Control Center and Lock Screen

Open Control Center, press and hold, tap Add a Control, search for Lavie and add Log spending. On the Lock Screen, customize it and add the same control or widget. It opens Lavie's keypad.

Action button

Settings › Action Button › Shortcut › Log spending from Lavie. Press it, type the amount, done.

Privacy

Alerts are read on your iPhone. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on the DeepSeek fallback, and then only the text of an alert Lavie couldn't read. See the privacy policy.
