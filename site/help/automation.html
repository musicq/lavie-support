Automatic logging

Pay with Apple Pay or get a bank alert, and the purchase lands in Lavie by itself, filed under the name's category.

It takes the Lavie shortcut, added once, and one automation for each source in the Shortcuts app. Lavie reads each alert on your iPhone. Codes, declined cards and balance alerts are ignored.

1. Add the Lavie shortcut

Log in Lavie

The only shortcut you need. Every source below runs it.

Get the shortcut

Open it on the iPhone that has Lavie. The page shows what to tap at each step.

2. Choose what runs it

Each source is an automation in the Shortcuts app: open Shortcuts, tap Automation at the bottom, then + (or New Automation the first time). Pick the trigger and choose Run Immediately so it never asks. Tap Next, then Log in Lavie under My Shortcuts. That's it.

Apple Pay (iOS 17 and later)

The amount and the shop, the moment you tap your iPhone or Apple Watch to pay. Only payments made by tapping; online purchases arrive through your bank's notifications instead.

  1. Shortcuts › Automation › + › Wallet (called Transaction on iOS 17).
  2. Pick the cards you pay with. Choose Run Immediately, then Next.
  3. Under My Shortcuts, pick Log in Lavie.

Bank or wallet app notifications (iOS 27 and later)

Card purchases, PayNow, Alipay, WeChat Pay: whatever your apps already notify you about.

  1. Shortcuts › Automation › + › Notification.
  2. Choose the bank or wallet app. Leave the text filter empty. Choose Run Immediately, then Next.
  3. Under My Shortcuts, pick Log in Lavie.

Repeat for each app you want Lavie to read. A notification that isn't about a purchase logs nothing.

Bank text messages

The SMS alerts many banks send for every card charge.

  1. Shortcuts › Automation › + › Message.
  2. Tap Message Contains and type a phrase your bank's alerts always use, such as "transaction of", "消费" or "ご利用". Bank numbers usually can't be chosen as a sender, so the phrase is what picks the right messages. Choose Run Immediately, then Next.
  3. Under My Shortcuts, pick Log in Lavie.

Card alerts by email

Cards that email you after each charge, as many do in Japan. Works with the Mail app.

  1. Shortcuts › Automation › + › Email.
  2. Pick the sender your card alerts come from. Choose Run Immediately, then Next.
  3. Under My Shortcuts, pick Log in Lavie.

3. Check what arrived

If Lavie may send you notifications, each automatic entry sends a note: tap it to fix the amount or name, or press and hold it and tap Undo. Settings › Automatic logging shows how many purchases have arrived so far.

If Lavie keeps misreading a bank's alerts, send us their wording (with the amounts changed) and we'll teach it. You can also turn on Ask AI to read it in Settings › Automatic logging for alerts Lavie can't read on its own: only that alert's text is sent, up to ten times a cycle on the free plan.

One tap, no automation

Control Center and Lock Screen

Open Control Center, press and hold an empty spot, tap Add a Control, search for Lavie and add Log spending. On the Lock Screen, press and hold, tap Customize, then add the same control or the Lavie widget. Each opens Lavie's keypad.

Action button

Settings › Action Button › Controls › search for Lavie › Log spending. Press the button and Lavie's keypad opens: type the amount, done.

Privacy

Alerts are read on your iPhone. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on Ask AI to read it, and then only the text of an alert Lavie couldn't read. See the privacy policy.

Stuck?

Write to support@lifebar.app with your iOS version and which step didn't work.
