Import bank records

Bring a file from your bank or wallet; Lavie finds the transactions and shows you the list before anything is added.

Statements come once a month, but the transaction list in your bank's app can be exported any day. Lavie skips what it already has, including your repeating bills, so nothing is counted twice.

What Lavie reads

  • PDF statements, including scanned ones.
  • CSV and Excel (.xlsx) exports, in English, Chinese (GB18030 and Big5), Japanese (Shift-JIS) and UTF-8.
  • Zip archives, including the password-protected ones Alipay and WeChat Pay send by email. Share the zip as it is; Lavie asks for the password and never stores it.
  • Screenshots and photos of a transaction list.

The file is read on your iPhone. Only its text is sent to DeepSeek, an AI service, to find the transactions, after you allow it once. Files over 30 MB are refused; very long statements are cut at about 120,000 characters, and Lavie says so.

Where to find this month's records

Singapore

DBS / POSB, OCBC, UOB: open the account or card in the app, find Transaction History, and look for the download or share icon. Accounts go back about six months; cards show the current month.

PayLah!, PayNow: the transaction list has a share icon that exports a PDF.

China

Alipay (支付宝): 我的 › 账单 › ··· › 开具交易流水证明 › 用于个人对账. Choose the dates and send it to your email. The zip is protected by the password in Alipay's message; share the zip to Lavie as it is and type the password when asked.

WeChat Pay (微信支付): 我 › 服务 › 钱包 › 账单 › 常见问题 › 下载账单 › 用于个人对账. Up to three months at a time, sent by email as a zip with a password.

Bank apps: look for 账单 or 交易明细 and an export that emails you the file.

Hong Kong and Taiwan

Most banking apps export the current month under 交易紀錄 or 帳單 as a CSV or PDF; e-wallets under 交易紀錄.

Japan

Cards (楽天カード, 三井住友カード, JCB, イオンカード): ご利用明細 › CSVダウンロード on the website, or a screenshot of the list in the app.

Banks and PayPay: 入出金明細 or 取引履歴 › ダウンロード, or a screenshot.

United States and Canada

Chase, Amex, Bank of America, Capital One, TD, RBC: Activity › Download, choose CSV and a date range up to today.

Apple Card: Wallet › Apple Card › Card Balance › a statement › Export Transactions.

Europe and the UK

Most banking apps export a CSV or PDF under Transactions (Umsätze, Mouvements, Movimientos) with a date range up to today. Revolut, N26, Monzo, Wise: Statement › Export.

Brazil

Nubank, Itaú, Bradesco, Inter: Extrato › Exportar, as CSV, OFX or PDF.

Anywhere else

Open the account in your bank's app, find the transaction list, and look for a download, export or share icon. A screenshot of the list works too.

After an import

Lavie remembers the last day the import covered and suggests exporting from there to today next time. If a name came back monthly for about the same amount (Netflix, the phone bill), Lavie offers to turn those entries into one repeating bill, so it's counted before payday. Everything an import adds can be undone from the message that appears afterwards.

Free plan

One import per payday cycle is free. Lavie Plus has no limit.

A file Lavie couldn't read?

Write to support@lifebar.app with the bank or app it came from and the file type. A sample with the amounts changed helps most.
