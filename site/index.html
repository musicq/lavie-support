01

Your payday,
not the calendar.

Every cycle runs from one payday to the next. If payday falls on a weekend, Lavie moves it to the day the money really lands, and says so.

02

Bills are counted
before they're due.

Rent, your phone, the gym: anything still to come this cycle is already taken out of your number. A big bill day is never a surprise.

03

Can I afford it?
Ask before you buy.

Type the price and get a straight answer: what you'd still have free until payday, and how much that leaves for each day.

04

Log it in
a second.

Tap +, type the amount, pick a name you've used before. Lavie remembers that “Lunch” is food, so you never sort anything by hand.
