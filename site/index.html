Am I okay until payday?

Lavie answers with one number: Free to spend, built from your payday, your income, your bills and your goals.

  • One number. What's left after the bills still due and the money you set aside for goals.
  • Your payday, not the calendar. Every cycle runs from one payday to the next.
  • Goals come first. Lavie sets money aside each payday so goals don't depend on what's left over.
  • Private by default. Your numbers stay on your iPhone, or in your own iCloud if you turn on sync.
