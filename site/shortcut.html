  4. 3

    Choose what runs it

    On iOS 27, open Log in Lavie › Edit › Automation and add a trigger. Choose a guide below to connect the right input and allow running when locked. Older iOS versions use the Automation tab.

What's inside

One step from Lavie: Log a purchase from Shortcut Input. It reads the text your automation passes in, such as a bank alert. When it recognizes a transaction, Lavie adds an entry under its name's category and sends you a note to check it. Use the setup guide to connect the source text, then check a new alert in Lavie's Recent runs.

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you turn on Ask AI to read it in Lavie, and then only the text of an alert Lavie couldn't read. See the privacy policy.

If it doesn't work

Nothing happened after Download? The file waits in Safari: tap the download button at the left of the address bar, then Downloads. You can also find it in the Files app, under Downloads.

Called “Log in Lavie 2”? Safari numbers a file it has downloaded before. It works the same; pick that one in your automations.

Still stuck? Write to support@lifebar.app with your iOS version and the step that didn't work.
