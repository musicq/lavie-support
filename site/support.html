Support

Using Lavie

What does "Free to spend" mean?

It's the money you can still spend until your next payday: the income in this cycle, minus what you've already spent, the bills still due before payday, and what Lavie sets aside for your goals.

How do I change my payday?

On the Plan tab, tap the payday at the top (or the gear › Your plan › Payday). Choose the day of the month, or the last day of the month, and what happens when it falls on a weekend: paid the Friday before, the Monday after, or on that day anyway.

How do I add a repeating bill or income?

On the Plan tab, choose Income or Bills and tap Add, then pick how often it repeats: monthly, weekly, every 2 weeks, quarterly, yearly, installments and more. From the keypad that + opens, "Set up as a repeating bill" takes what you typed to the same form.

How do I stop or change a bill?

Tap it on the Plan tab. To stop it, open More options › Ends on a date. A new price applies from the next charge, so what you've already paid stays as it was. Lavie also reminds you the evening before each bill, and you can stop it right from that reminder.

How do goals work?

Give a goal a target and a month to reach it by. Lavie works out the same amount to set aside each payday and takes it out before your Free to spend.

Can I use more than one currency?

Choose your base currency in Settings › Your plan. With Lavie Plus, entries can be in any other currency too; they're converted with daily European Central Bank rates (through the Frankfurter service).

Can Lavie log purchases for me?

Yes. Add the Lavie shortcut, then let Apple Pay, your bank's notifications, texts or emails run it. See Automatic logging.

How does importing bank records work?

Choose a PDF, CSV or Excel file, a zip from Alipay or WeChat Pay, or screenshots. Lavie reads the file on your device and, with your permission, sends only its text to an AI service to find the transactions. You review everything before it's added, and anything Lavie already has is skipped. See Import bank records.

Can I share a ledger with someone?

Yes. Tap the ledgers button at the top left of Now, make a new ledger and invite people through iCloud. Each person can edit or only view, and your personal ledger stays separate. Inviting needs Lavie Plus and iCloud sync; joining someone else's ledger is free.

Does Lavie work on iPad?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad. Turn on iCloud sync (Lavie Plus) to keep both the same.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, Lavie also keeps a copy in your own private iCloud account. Lavie has no accounts and keeps none of your records on its own servers.

How do I back up or move my data?

Go to Settings › Your data › Export a backup to save a complete JSON copy. To restore it, use Import a backup, then tap Merge (add it to what's there) or slide to Replace (make Lavie exactly the backup). Backups are free.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. If you used iCloud sync, also remove Lavie's data in the Settings app › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage. A shared ledger you own can be deleted from its settings in Lavie; one you joined can be left there.

Lavie Plus

What's included?

Widgets, a look ahead at your next three paydays, cycle reviews, unlimited goals and currencies, iCloud sync, your full Insights history and the year view, unlimited "Can I afford it?" checks and statement imports, inviting people to a shared ledger, your own categories, and unlimited AI tidies and alert reading.

What does the free plan include?

Free to spend with the full breakdown, your pay and bills, one running goal, three "Can I afford it?" checks each cycle, Insights for the current period and the three before it, one statement import each cycle, one AI tidy, and backups.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, or manage subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. If you're on a free trial, Lavie reminds you 2 days before it ends.

I bought Lavie Plus but it isn't showing

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account, then open Settings in Lavie and tap Restore Purchases in the Lavie Plus card at the top.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Contact us

Questions, feedback or a bug to report? Write to support@lifebar.app. It helps to include your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.
